Share the reunion

The family link, three text messages ready to copy, and a flyer with a QR code. Everything on this page is made in your browser; nothing is sent anywhere.

1. The family link

The link that opens the registration page. It carries the family code, so share it with Lewis family only. If the code ever changes, come back here and everything below updates.

2. Text messages

Copy one, paste it into a text or a group chat, and send. The plain ones use only characters every phone shows the same way. The styled one looks best in iMessage, WhatsApp and Messenger.

Plain

The whole story in one message.


      
Short
One line and the link. Good for a reminder.

      
Styled
The same news with some flourish. No emojis.
