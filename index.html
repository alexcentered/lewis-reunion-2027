Why we're doing this

Family can be ten buildings away.

Two cousins spent years working on the same base in San Antonio, about ten buildings apart, without knowing they were family. By the time they found out, they had about a year together before one was transferred to Japan.

Another time a cousin was stranded, and a chain of relatives got her picked up at the airport and home safely. Later, family in Dallas–Fort Worth gave her a place at their Thanksgiving table.

Registering for this reunion is the start of a family directory we keep after July, so we always know where our people are.

Questions

Good to know

How do I pay?

Pay the committee using the method they post here. Then go to My Registration and report the payment. Your balance updates once the committee's payment verifier confirms the money arrived. A reported payment shows as “awaiting confirmation” until then.

I married into the family. Should I register?

Yes. Choose “Married into the family” for your branch, and list your spouse so we can place you on the tree together.

I'm not sure which branch I'm from.

Choose “Not sure yet” and tell us your parents' and grandparents' names. A family tree captain will help you find your place.

My last name isn't Lewis or Antone.

That's fine. We record your current last name and your birth surname separately, so a Green, a Williams or an Arnwine stays connected to the Lewis line.

Who can see what I enter?

Your contact details, attendees and payments are visible only to you and the reunion committee. The “Who's coming” list shows only your household name, branch and city, and only if you tick that box. Stories, tributes and photos you share are visible to family members who have this page.

Getting here

Please arrange your own travel to Fresno and between the hotel and venues. Fresno Yosemite International Airport (FAT) is the closest airport.
