Lewis Family Reunion 2027

The registration pages: your words

The Register page, My Registration and the emails they send, drawn the way families see them. Change any words, and leave a note on any part.

How this works
  1. Tap or click any words on the pages and emails below and type over them. Words you change turn green.
  2. Changed your mind? Tap the words again and press Put back. Each part also has Put back today’s words for all its changes.
  3. Words in [square brackets] are filled in by the website for each family, such as a name, an email or an amount. Keep the brackets where those should go.
  4. Under every part is a note box for anything else: something to add, take out or move. Two general notes boxes are at the end.
  5. There is nothing to send. Everything saves by itself, and Alex sees it right away.

These are drawings of the pages, so the boxes and buttons in them don’t work. Nothing changes on the real website until the committee decides.
