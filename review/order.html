The homepage: what order?

This is the homepage in the order from your email, with the new sections that are on the site now. Move things until it looks right.

Reading this on a phone or in an email preview? You can see the sections here, but the buttons (moving, Email, Copy) only work when the file is saved and opened in a web browser on a computer, such as Chrome, Safari or Edge. Or just reply to Alex's email with the order you would like.

How this works
  1. Drag a section up or down to change the order, or use the ↑ ↓ buttons.
  2. Don't want a section on the homepage? Drag it down to Not on the homepage, or press Remove. To bring one in, drag it up or press Add.
  3. To change a section's wording, press Edit words and type over it. The preview shows your words as you type.
  4. Want to say something else about a section? Press Note.
  5. There is nothing to send. Your changes save by themselves, and Alex sees them right away.
