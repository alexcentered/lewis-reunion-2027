Why we're doing this

Family can be ten buildings away.

Two cousins spent years working on the same base in San Antonio, about ten buildings apart, without knowing they were family. By the time they found out, they had about a year together before one was transferred to Japan.

Another time a cousin was stranded, and a chain of relatives got her picked up at the airport and home safely. Later, family in Dallas–Fort Worth gave her a place at their Thanksgiving table.

Welcome

Welcome to the Lewis Family Roots Website.

A website rooted in our family legacy and its generational strengths… If you are here, it's because you belong. If you are here, it is because you matter, and your presence is a testament to the branch of the Lewis Family roots you are part of and have contributed to.

A place that is rooted in “The Bottom” of Mobile, Alabama, that has spanned the globe. A place with humble beginnings, born of John and Anna Mary Lewis and the 13 children they brought into this world. This place is designed to be generational in nature, with a torch passing to maintain our legacy through the generations of aunts, uncles, and the vast number of cousins, 1st, 2nd, 3rd, removed, and beyond. We are the Lewis family.

Please continue to come back, as this website will evolve to include your pictures, your stories, an interactive family tree, and a marketplace. Its design is to grow through the years with videos, testimonials, and memorials of our past loved ones. Its design is intentional because you matter!

Thank you for stopping by.

The 2027 Family Reunion

Our first order of business.

Our first order of business for this site is to intentionally plan a get-together, but not just any get-together, a Reunion. A reunion that is internal in its purpose. It's true, we as a family have gotten together for a funeral and made some memories there, but those get-togethers we respectfully designed to send our loved ones off to heaven, where we know a Lewis party is continually being held. But this reunion is to reach the family members on all coasts, and all countries, with the one purpose of gathering, sharing our stories, meeting the old and new generations of our families, to learn from each other, to share the successes and failures, to plant seeds for our legacy, and to purposefully unite our family to make us strong. It's true that pictures are an amazing way to see the triumphs of our family, but nothing, and I say nothing, takes away the physical presence of seeing your family member, being able to hug them, cry with them, and love them, and that’s the purpose of the 2027 Family Reunion.

It is planned in Fresno, California, where our oldest living Lewis resides. It will span 4 days, from Friday, 2 July 2027 through Monday, 5 July 2027, and will offer plenty of opportunities to connect, share, laugh, and pay homage to the roots of the 13 generations of families who have contributed to the hundreds of branches that make up the Lewis Family.
