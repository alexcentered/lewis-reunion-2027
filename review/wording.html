How this works
  1. Answer the decisions near the top: the payment due date and a few other choices.
  2. Read each email below. The grey box is what it says today.
  3. To change a line, type your wording in the box under it. Leave a box empty to keep today's words.
  4. There is nothing to send. Your answers save by themselves, and Alex sees them right away.

Words in blue are filled in by the website for each family (names, links, codes, amounts). You don't need to write those. Everything you type is saved, so you can close the page and come back later with the same link, on this device or another one.

Subject

Welcome to the Lewis Family Reunion 2027

Welcome

On behalf of the reunion committee, welcome. From Friday, July 2 to Monday, July 5, 2027, the Lewis family is gathering in Fresno, California, and we want you there.

The weekend

It is four days to connect and reconnect: a meet and greet on Friday evening, a banquet on Saturday night, family day and a picnic on Sunday with Fourth of July fireworks, and a farewell breakfast on Monday morning. The schedule on the website fills in as plans are confirmed.

Day by day (designed version)

Fri, Jul 2: Hotel check-in, then a meet and greet in the evening at Clo and Aunt Do’s Place Sat, Jul 3: Reunion activities and a family photo with every branch, then the banquet at the Dante Club Sun, Jul 4: Family day and picnic at Frasier Gardens, and Fourth of July fireworks Mon, Jul 5: Farewell breakfast at Tessy’s house, then safe travels home
